Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Filmora. We value your trust and are fully committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, manage, use, and process data when you access our video streaming platform ecosystem, subscriptions, and website interfaces.

2. Information We Collect

To provide you with a highly customized cinematic and web experience, we gather data elements across two main tracks:

  • Account Information: Profile credentials, names, email addresses, and secure authentication metadata generated during login configurations.
  • Payment & Subscriptions Data: Processing transaction details through safe payment channels to maintain active tier selections (Basic, Standard, or Premium).
  • Usage & Telemetry Details: Streaming logs, content selection watch history, device information, browser footprints, and active UI preferences.

3. How We Use Your Data

Filmora leverages the compiled information arrays strictly to sustain operational stability, specifically including:

Enhancing personalization algorithms to suggest relevant media matching your unique history, running structural telemetry to minimize playback latency, and safely identifying user sessions via our toggle authentication components.

4. Your Control Rights

You preserve full control rights over your digital workspace environment. You can update your profile parameters at any moment, manage cookie tracking configurations directly via your device browser properties, or instantly log out of sessions using the interactive dropdown configuration.

5. Contact Support

Should you have any persistent inquiries or require technical details regarding our overarching privacy execution framework, please do not hesitate to contact our dedicated customer management team through our support portal channels.
